Minnowly - Terms of Use

This is an English translation for convenience. In case of any difference, the Turkish version applies.

1. General

Minnowly is a mobile app that offers a catch journal, weather, a fishing regulations guide, a shared map and messaging between users. By using the app you accept these terms. Even without an account you can use the catch journal on your own phone only.

2. Account

A valid email address is required to create an account. You are responsible for your account; do not share your password. You can permanently delete your account at any time in Settings.

3. Content you share

The catch details, photo, location and display name you share on the shared map are visible to all signed-in users. You must only share photos you took yourself and your own catches. You can remove a share by deleting the catch. By sharing content you give Minnowly a limited permission to display it for the purpose of operating the app.

4. Prohibited content and behavior

It is forbidden to share: unlawful, insulting, hateful or sexual content; other people's personal data or photos; misleading or fake catch information; spam; and anything that encourages illegal fishing (closed species, closed seasons or fish under the legal size). You can report inappropriate content with the "Report" button under each catch and hide users with "Block". Content that breaks the rules may be removed; accounts may be closed for repeated violations.

5. Fishing regulations and liability

The species, size and closed-season information in the app is a summary compiled from the official Fisheries Communiqué for information purposes and may be out of date. The binding rules are the current legislation published by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry. Weather and "fishing score" information are estimates; safety decisions at sea and on the shore are yours. The app is provided "as is"; uninterrupted or error-free operation is not guaranteed.

6. Location safety

If you are signed in, catches to which you add a location are shared on the shared map with their exact location and are visible to other users. Do not add a location to catches you do not want to share. Do not share dangerous, private or restricted areas.

7. Messaging

Signed-in users can send each other one-to-one messages. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Harassment, threats, insults, spam, and sexual or unlawful content are forbidden. You can report an offensive message by long-pressing it and choosing "Report", and block a user from the menu in the chat; reported messages may be reviewed. You can edit a message within 15 minutes of sending it. Messages you write in the Public Chat room are visible to all signed-in users; do not share personal information. Accounts may be closed for violations. You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings.

8. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated; important changes are announced in the app. For questions and notices: uygunapps@gmail.com
