Minnowly - Privacy Policy and KVKK Notice

This is an English translation for convenience. In case of any difference, the Turkish version applies.

Data controller

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the data controller is Uygun Apps. Contact: uygunapps@gmail.com

What data we process

Kept only on your phone (even without an account): your catch records, spots, photos and settings. These are not sent to us.

If you create an account: your email address, display name and password (the password is stored in encrypted form).

If you are signed in, for catches with a location: species, length, weight, bait, gear, catch time, place name, the exact location you chose and, if you added one, the photo.

If you use messaging: the content and time of messages you send and receive, edit times, and the times you read and delete chats. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Messages you write in the Public Chat room and your display name are visible to all signed-in users.

If you allow notifications: your phone's notification address (Firebase Cloud Messaging token).

Your report and block records (including the catches and messages you reported).

Purpose and legal basis

Account management, operating the shared map, messaging and sending new-message notifications, preventing abuse, and security. Legal bases: establishment and performance of a contract, legitimate interest and your explicit consent. When you create an account and add a location to a catch, that catch is shared on the shared map; if you do not add a location, the catch stays only on your phone. You can remove a share by deleting the catch.

Location data

Your device location is used on your phone to show you on the map and to fetch the weather. It is sent to the weather service (Open-Meteo) with about 100 meters of precision. Your location goes to the shared map only when you are signed in and add a location to a catch, with the exact location you chose. No background location tracking is done.

Where data is stored and transfers

Account and shared data are stored on the servers of our service provider Supabase abroad, so your data is transferred outside Turkey. The transfer is based on the explicit consent you give when creating an account and on what is necessary to provide the service. Map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap servers (your IP address may be visible to them). We do not sell your data to third parties for advertising. To send notifications, your notification address, the sender's name and the first part of the message pass through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service. We do not read your messages unless they are reported.

Retention

When you delete your account, your account, profile, all catches and photos you shared, your chats and all messages in them are deleted from the server. "Delete chat" only hides the chat from your own inbox; it remains visible to the other person. You can edit a message within 15 minutes of sending it. Your catch journal on your phone is not deleted; you can delete it by uninstalling the app.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, request correction or deletion, object to processing and claim compensation for damages. You can delete your account in the app and write to uygunapps@gmail.com for other requests. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.

Children

Minnowly is not intended for children under 13. You must be at least 13 to create an account; users under 18 must get the consent of a parent or guardian when creating an account.
